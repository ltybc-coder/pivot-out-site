Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Pivot Out: Arrow Logic and these support pages handle information. Pivot Out is an independent iPhone puzzle game. For support or privacy requests, contact its developer at ltybc.coder@gmail.com.

You do not need to create an account to play. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Progress on your device

Your campaign progress, coins, unlocked board styles, Daily Pivot history, tutorial state, and settings are stored locally on your device. Game Center support starts with build 1.0 (4); the cloud features below apply only to builds with the Game Center button. Earlier builds keep gameplay progress locally. Device backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Settings → Reset Game Progress clears gameplay progress after confirmation. Sound, haptics, analytics preferences, video-ad permission, and the Remove Ads purchase are kept. Deleting the app removes its local app data; it does not delete iCloud saves or Game Center records. Backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Game Center and iCloud

In builds that support it, connecting from the Home screen is optional. The game then uses Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboard scores, and iCloud Drive for a complete gameplay save: campaign level, coins, mastery, Daily history, board styles, and tutorial completion. It remembers your connection and reconnects on later launches. Playing without connecting remains available.

The app uses a Game Center player identifier to keep account-specific local progress and pending records separate. A digest of that identifier names the iCloud save. These identifiers and save files are not sent to our analytics or advertising providers. Settings stay on the device; Remove Ads ownership is verified separately through StoreKit.

Apple associates Game Center activity with your account. Your Game Center nickname, avatar, and leaderboard scores may be visible to other players. Apple provides activity-sharing controls in Settings → Game Center. See Game Center & Privacy and Apple's privacy policy.

Cloud sync needs the same Game Center and iCloud accounts on your devices and iCloud Drive enabled. Sync applies when you return to Home. If both devices changed a save, you choose one complete version; balances and owned styles are not combined. A connected Reset Game Progress replaces the synced gameplay save with reset progress while keeping Game Center achievements, record scores, and Apple purchases. Signing out of Game Center stops this app's Game Center sync; it does not erase existing Apple records. Apple manages cloud storage and account privacy controls. We do not keep a separate copy on a developer-operated server.

Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay analytics is off until you choose Share Analytics. Choosing Don’t Share does not prevent you from playing. You can change the choice in Settings → Anonymous gameplay analytics.

When enabled, Pivot Out sends events to TelemetryDeck to understand difficulty, reliability, and how features are used. Events include session starts and duration, screens viewed, tutorial steps, level attempts and results, blocked moves, hints, recovery, coin transactions, purchase attempts and outcomes, Remove Ads ownership status, ad-break attempts and outcomes, ad loads and impressions, and settings changes. They may include level identifiers, difficulty, move counts, and app version. The SDK adds device and operating-system information and an anonymized identifier. We do not attach your name, email, message text, or payment details to these events.

TelemetryDeck describes how it anonymizes identifiers and states that it does not store IP addresses in its analytics service. See its Privacy FAQ and Privacy Policy.

Turning analytics off stops new event collection and clears pending SDK data in the app. It does not recall information already delivered. Anonymized events may be retained by TelemetryDeck without a fixed deletion schedule, as described in its Privacy FAQ; they may not be identifiable from your email address.

Advertising and your choices

In builds with video rewards enabled, Pivot Out uses Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and Unity Ad Quality. You choose when to watch a video for a hint, undo, restored focus, or extra coins. Builds with campaign ad breaks also request a full-screen ad when you leave the result of levels 5, 10, 15, and so on, unless you own Remove Ads. Breaks do not interrupt a puzzle or appear in Daily Pivot. If an ad is unavailable, play continues. The game requests non-personalized ads and sends privacy signals that decline personalized advertising and opt out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing.

Starting with build 1.0 (3), the first video-reward offer explains these services beside Watch Ad. Choosing Watch Ad saves your permission and starts that request, without a separate permission pop-up. Closing the offer, choosing Not now, or using coins does not grant permission. Build 1.0 (2) uses a separate Allow video ads prompt. Advertising services do not start until you make an affirmative choice. In builds with campaign ad breaks, the disclosure covers both optional videos and those breaks. An older permission covering optional videos alone does not enable automatic breaks: the expanded choice is requested before the first break, or alongside a later Watch Ad offer. An existing refusal remains in effect. This choice is separate from gameplay analytics.

When enabled, these services may process IP address and approximate location, device or installation identifiers where permitted, device and app information, ad views and interactions, and performance and diagnostic information. This supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and ad-quality monitoring. The game does not ask for your precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone to show ads. Our release flow does not request Apple's tracking permission.

Use Settings → Ad privacy choices → Turn off video ads to block new ad requests. Close and reopen the app to end an advertising session that has already started. Your choice is saved on this device and survives Reset Game Progress. Turning ads off does not delete information already received by a provider. You can keep playing with the available free or coin-based options.

Unity explains its processing, retention, international transfers, and access or deletion options in its Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. You can also use privacy controls within a Unity ad when available. For help with a request, contact the developer at the address below.

Advertising availability varies by build. Earlier TestFlight build 0.1.0 (1) included Google's Mobile Ads and consent libraries with live ads disabled. Its provider information remains available in Google's SDK disclosure. Separate development builds may use test ads and keep their video-ad permission separately from the App Store app.

Remove Ads purchase

In version 1.0, Remove Ads is an optional one-time purchase through Apple. It includes video rewards without watching ads or spending coins, and disables campaign ad breaks in builds that include them. Apple processes payments using your Apple Account. The game uses StoreKit to verify and restore your purchase; it does not send receipts, transaction identifiers, or payment details to the developer or advertising services.

If gameplay analytics is enabled, purchase status and events such as purchase success, cancellation, or pending approval may be sent to TelemetryDeck without payment details. Restore Purchases is available in Shop and Settings. Reset Game Progress does not remove this purchase. Apple manages refunds and purchase history under Apple’s privacy policy.

Diagnostics and TestFlight

Apple’s MetricKit may provide performance and crash reports to the app. Pivot Out keeps only the 20 newest report files locally and does not upload these raw files through its own code. When gameplay analytics is enabled, it may send counts of reports, crashes, hangs, and resource exceptions to TelemetryDeck.

Apple may separately provide TestFlight feedback and app diagnostics under Apple’s terms and your device or testing settings. See Apple’s privacy information.

When you contact support

If you email the developer, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send, such as a message or screenshot. The in-app report includes app version, iOS version, and device model; a level report also includes the level number, size, difficulty, generation seed, and content fingerprint. You can review the report before sending. Reports are not sent automatically.

Support messages are handled through Gmail to answer questions and investigate problems. Correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request and for relevant follow-up or legal obligations. Contact the same address to request deletion. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

These pages and service providers

These pages do not add advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Unity, TelemetryDeck, Apple, Google (support email and older builds), and GitHub process information under their respective policies. Depending on the service, processing may take place outside your country. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Your choices and requests

Use the in-app controls to change analytics preferences, review available ad privacy choices, or reset gameplay progress (including its connected cloud save). Depending on your location and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority.

For requests concerning information held by the developer, email ltybc.coder@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify the correspondence involved. We cannot identify individual anonymized gameplay events by an email address or restore deleted local progress.

Where applicable, optional analytics is based on your choice, support correspondence is used to respond to your request, and service security and required records are handled for those purposes. Permission for optional video ads is managed separately in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Provider privacy and deletion controls are described in the advertising section above.

Children

Pivot Out is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

Pivot Out does not ask players to submit personal details inside the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, contact the support address so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate. Parents can manage app access and device sharing settings through Apple’s controls.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Questions about this policy can be sent to ltybc.coder@gmail.com.
