The short version. No game account is required. Optional analytics helps improve the puzzles. Support reports are sent only when you choose to send them.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Pivot Out: Arrow Logic and these support pages handle information. Pivot Out is an independent iPhone puzzle game. For support or privacy requests, contact its developer at ltybc.coder@gmail.com.

You do not need to create an account to play. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

Progress on your device

Your campaign progress, coins, unlocked board styles, Daily Pivot history, tutorial state, and settings are stored locally on your device. The current version does not sync your game through Game Center or iCloud. Any device backups you choose to make are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Settings → Reset Game Progress clears gameplay progress after confirmation. Sound, haptics, and analytics preferences are kept. Deleting the app removes its local app data; backups may retain copies according to your backup settings.

Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay analytics is off until you choose Share Analytics. Choosing Don’t Share does not prevent you from playing. You can change the choice in Settings → Anonymous gameplay analytics.

When enabled, Pivot Out sends events to TelemetryDeck to understand difficulty, reliability, and how features are used. Events include session starts and duration, screens viewed, tutorial steps, level attempts and results, blocked moves, hints, recovery, coin transactions, and settings changes. They may include level identifiers, difficulty, move counts, and app version. The SDK adds device and operating-system information and an anonymized identifier. We do not attach your name, email, message text, or payment details to these events.

TelemetryDeck describes how it anonymizes identifiers and states that it does not store IP addresses in its analytics service. See its Privacy FAQ and Privacy Policy.

Turning analytics off stops new event collection and clears pending SDK data in the app. It does not recall information already delivered. Anonymized events may be retained by TelemetryDeck without a fixed deletion schedule, as described in its Privacy FAQ; they may not be identifiable from your email address.

Optional advertising

Live advertising is disabled in the current TestFlight build. Versions with advertising enabled use Google AdMob for player-initiated video rewards. The app requests non-personalized ads and disables Google’s publisher first-party ID feature. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

When these services are enabled, Google’s advertising and consent tools may process your IP address (including an approximate location derived from it), device identifiers where permitted, ad views and interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Google’s consent form is shown where required before requesting an ad. When available, use Ad privacy choices in Settings to review your preferences. Gameplay analytics consent and advertising consent are separate. You can decline an optional video reward and continue with other available gameplay options.

Diagnostics and TestFlight

Apple’s MetricKit may provide performance and crash reports to the app. Pivot Out keeps only the 20 newest report files locally and does not upload these raw files through its own code. When gameplay analytics is enabled, it may send counts of reports, crashes, hangs, and resource exceptions to TelemetryDeck.

Apple may separately provide TestFlight feedback and app diagnostics under Apple’s terms and your device or testing settings. See Apple’s privacy information.

When you contact support

If you email the developer, we receive your email address and anything you choose to send, such as a message or screenshot. The in-app report includes app version, iOS version, and device model; a level report also includes the level number, size, difficulty, generation seed, and content fingerprint. You can review the report before sending. Reports are not sent automatically.

Support messages are handled through Gmail to answer questions and investigate problems. Correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request and for relevant follow-up or legal obligations. Contact the same address to request deletion. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

These pages and service providers

These pages do not add advertising, analytics scripts, contact forms, or tracking cookies. They are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including for visitors who are not signed in. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Apple, Google, TelemetryDeck, and the website hosting providers process information under their respective policies. Depending on the service, processing may take place outside your country. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Your choices and requests

Use the in-app controls to change analytics preferences, review available ad privacy choices, or reset local progress. Depending on your location and the information involved, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, object to or restrict its use, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority.

For requests concerning information held by the developer, email ltybc.coder@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify the correspondence involved. We cannot identify individual anonymized gameplay events by an email address or restore deleted local progress.

Where applicable, optional analytics is based on your choice, support correspondence is used to respond to your request, and service security and required records are handled for those purposes. Advertising consent is managed separately through the available Google consent controls.

Children

Pivot Out does not ask players to submit personal details inside the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, contact the support address so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate. Parents can manage app access and device sharing settings through Apple’s controls.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the app’s data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Questions about this policy can be sent to ltybc.coder@gmail.com.
