The best way to report a level

Send the puzzle with your message.

Use Report Current Level in the game. It prepares the level number and the details needed to recreate that exact puzzle. You can review everything before sending.

For other questions, open Settings → Help & Support. If email is unavailable, use Share or Copy and send the report to the address above.

What helps us investigate

A few details go a long way.

  1. What happened, and what you expected instead.
  2. The level number, or whether you were playing Daily Pivot.
  3. Your app version and iPhone model. The in-app report adds basic technical details for you.
  4. A screenshot or short recording, if it shows the problem. Leave out any private information.
Quick answers

Before your next move.

How does Remove Ads work?

In version 1.0, the optional one-time Remove Ads purchase includes extra hints, Undo, continue, and double campaign rewards without videos or spending coins. In builds with campaign ad breaks, it also disables the full-screen ads after every fifth campaign level. Continue is available once per attempt and doubling once per campaign result. Buy it from Shop or Settings. To recover an existing purchase, use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account. Reset Game Progress keeps this purchase. If an offer is temporarily unavailable, try loading the price again later.

Why does removing an arrow change the board?

An arrow can leave when its path is clear. Removing it rotates its neighboring arrows clockwise, so each exit changes which moves are possible next. Watch the neighboring arrows before choosing your move.

I reached a dead end. What can I do?

Use the free recovery offered by the game to return to a position with a verified route to a win, or restart the level. If recovery still leaves you stuck, send a Report Current Level message and describe the moves that led there.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is always saved on your device. Starting with build 1.0 (4), builds with the Game Center button also support optional iCloud sync. Connect from Home, use the same Game Center and iCloud accounts on both devices, and enable iCloud Drive. Return to Home and wait for “Progress saved in iCloud” before changing devices. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting. Separate development builds may have separate saves.

Why does the game ask me to choose a save?

This can happen when you play on two devices before they sync. Compare the level, coins, Daily progress, and styles, then choose the complete save you want to keep. The game does not combine coin balances or styles from different saves. Choose Later to keep playing locally and decide from the cloud status on Home when you are ready.

Does resetting progress clear Game Center?

A connected Reset Game Progress also resets the gameplay save in iCloud. Game Center achievements and leaderboard records remain, and Remove Ads stays linked to your Apple purchase. Deleting the app alone does not clear cloud saves. Switching Game Center accounts uses separate cached progress for each account.

Can I play without an internet connection?

The campaign and Daily Pivot work offline. Game Center and cloud sync, optional video rewards, sending email, and opening web pages need an internet connection. An unavailable ad does not grant a reward; free restart and the available coin options remain usable.

When do campaign ad breaks appear?

In builds with campaign ad breaks, a full-screen ad is requested when you leave the result of levels 5, 10, 15, and so on. Remove Ads disables these breaks. Ads do not interrupt a puzzle or appear in Daily Pivot. Advertising must be enabled in your privacy choices, and a video must be available; otherwise you can continue playing. Upgrading from a build with optional videos asks for the expanded permission before enabling automatic breaks.

Why is a video reward unavailable?

Ads may be disabled in your build or unavailable because of your connection, privacy choices, or ad availability. Earlier TestFlight build 0.1.0 (1) has live advertising disabled. If an ad appears but behaves incorrectly, use Settings → Report an Ad. Rewards are granted only when the ad provider confirms completion.

How do I turn video ads off?

In builds with video rewards, open Settings → Ad privacy choices → Turn off video ads. This blocks new requests. Close and reopen the app to end an advertising session already in progress. The in-game Not now choice only skips the current request. Starting with build 1.0 (3), the first Watch Ad offer explains data processing inline; there is no second permission pop-up for that reward request. Builds with campaign ad breaks explain both formats, and may ask for the expanded choice before the first break if it has not already been made. A saved settings refusal stays in effect until you change it in Settings. Advertising permission is separate from gameplay analytics.

How do I change analytics or reset my game?

Turn Anonymous gameplay analytics off in Settings to stop new gameplay analytics. Reset Game Progress clears campaign progress, coins, mastery, Daily history, palettes, and tutorial progress after confirmation. It keeps your sound, haptics, analytics, and video-ad choices. Resetting the game does not delete information already sent to an analytics or advertising provider.

When does Daily Pivot change?

A new Daily Pivot is available each day at midnight UTC, which may be a different time in your time zone. Daily Pivot unlocks after the first two tutorial levels. You can retry the daily board; its coin reward is granted only for the first clear that day.
